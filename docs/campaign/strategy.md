# Campaign Strategy: Build First (v0.1)

Depends on `offer-mechanics.md` v0.2 and `audience-research.md`. Numbers marked **target** are planning goals to be replaced by measured baselines after week 2. They are not benchmarks and never appear publicly.

## 1. Positioning

**For** companies with aging stacks, teams that need automation, and founders with an idea, **Monoes is the build partner** that builds first against a written Acceptance Spec and gets paid only if the build meets it.

**Why we can say it:** the Spec makes "done" objective; the build sits in staging; the terms are fixed before work starts; and Monoes publishes open-source tooling (Monomind, Mono Agent) anyone can inspect.

**What we are not:** a free-work gimmick, a consultancy that sells hours (Workforce is a separate service that starts with research paid after delivery), or an agency that sells hours. Say so plainly.

Primary lines, in priority order: **Modernize** (stack modernization) > **Flow** (automation) > **Launch** (product development). Lead the business page with Modernize; lead the startup page with Launch.

## 3. Funnel

| Stage | What happens | Owner / system | Primary metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Organic posts, community answers, outreach, search pages | Social + monoagent drafts | Qualified visits |
| Application | Form on the landing page | Next.js form + database | Applications / qualified visits |
| Qualification | Gate review within 2 business days | Founder or delegate | Qualified / applications |
| Spec call | 60-90 minute call, Spec drafted | Delivery lead | Spec calls booked and completed |
| Spec signed | Frozen v1.0, pre-agreed terms | Founder | Signed / calls |
| Build | Staging build in window | Freelancers + Monoes review | On-time delivery |
| Review | Client runs the checklist | Client | Pass rate per row, time to decision |
| Conversion | Conversion Agreement or Build Fee or not accepted | Founder | Pass-and-sign rate |

## 5. Six-week calendar (week 1 starts after Phase 3 pages ship)

| Week | Focus | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation | Publish both pages and Terms page; verify forms and analytics; create suppression list; submit sitemap; founder long post on LinkedIn and Indie Hackers; 5 customer-discovery conversations booked per audience |
| 2 | Warm network | Founder posts on LinkedIn/X; direct messages to warm contacts (approved one by one); Reddit value posts in relevant communities (rules checked per subreddit); baseline metrics recorded |
| 3 | Content + community | Two proof-style posts (sample Spec walkthrough, "how a Spec row is written"); answer questions in public communities; first outreach sequences approved and started at warm-up volume |
| 4 | Launch moment | Product Hunt-style launch post and Indie Hackers launch thread (only if at least one slot is genuinely open); outreach at planned volume |
| 5 | Learn | Review rejected and lost applications; interview 3-5 drop-offs; test two headline variants per page |
| 6 | Decide | Compute funnel and economics; decide scale, pause or change; publish an honest update post |

| Search (guides) | Long-term | Existing guides section; add 2 to 3 modernization and Spec-writing guides later (separate task) |
| Email outreach | Limited | German/EU law generally requires prior consent for email advertising, including B2B. Use email only for (a) people who asked, (b) the waitlist, (c) non-EU leads under CAN-SPAM-style rules, subject to legal review |
| Open-source community | Credibility | GitHub README link to the offer; no hype |

## 7. Honesty checklist (applies to every asset)

- No invented clients, testimonials, logos, numbers, "as seen in".
- Slot count and waitlist position shown only if read from the database.
- "Terms apply" link next to every "pay only if" claim; the one-line pass-then-decline disclosure on each page.
- Equity deals never described as free.
- Freelancer delivery is never described as "in-house".
- Every message has an opt-out and respects the suppression list.

## 8. Risks

1. Uneconomic builds if conversion is low: pilot-first, effort cap.
2. Low trust with no references: lean on public repos, sample Spec, open terms; add case studies as they exist.
3. Legal exposure on advertising and equity: lawyer review before launch (offer doc, section 9).
4. Freelancer quality: Monoes review gate before staging delivery.
5. Account bans from outreach: conservative volumes, human approval, warm-up (Phase 4).
